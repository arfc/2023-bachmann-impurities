\opening{Dear Professor Hassan,}

Please find enclosed a revision of the manuscript entitled: ``Effects of uranium impurities 
in downblended HEU on advanced reactor performance'' which I and my coauthors, 
Richter and Munk, 
are submitting for exclusive consideration for publication as a research 
article in Nuclear Technology. 

This manuscript describes neutronic and reactor physics analysis of two advanced 
reactor designs when using uranium from difference sources. The work aims to 
identify potential limitations in using downblended highly enriched uranium (HEU) 
in these reactor designs, caused by uranium impurities not often found when enriching 
natural uranium to produce the fuel, by comparing different reactor physics 
parameters when using various fuel compositions. This work is motivated by the current 
efforts 
to use HEU inventories to produce high assay low enriched uranium (HALEU) to fuel 
advanced reactor demonstration projects in lieu of a developed HALEU supply chain based 
on enriching natural uranium. 

Thank you for your consideration of this work. I expect it will be of interest
to a broad readership concerned with advanced reactors, reactor physics, the nuclear 
fuel cycle, and Monte Carlo simulations.  Please address correspondence concerning
this manuscript to me.
